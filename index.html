    How it works

    Press play, start a focus session, and get to work. The music lowers itself when it is time for a break and comes back when you do. Free, no signup, nothing to install.

    All music on Custombiztool Focus Radio is original. Shortcuts: Space play or pause music, N next track, T start or pause the timer. The timer keeps time in a background tab, and the countdown shows in the tab title. Your settings are remembered on this device.
